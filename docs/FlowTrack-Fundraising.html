1. The Problem

Distributed and hybrid teams struggle to answer three simple questions — and stitch together 4–5 tools to try.

Where did the time go? Manual timesheets are inaccurate; managers lack real visibility into remote work.
Is the work real & billable? Agencies and outsourcing firms can't easily prove work to clients, causing payment disputes.
Too many disconnected tools. Time tracking, monitoring, payroll, invoicing and reporting live in separate products, wasting money and admin hours.

2. The Solution — FlowTrack

One platform that turns tracked time into payroll, invoices, and provable results — enhanced with AI. All of the following is already built and live:

Core

  • Time tracking + desktop app (Windows & macOS)
  • Screenshots & activity monitoring (with advanced mode)
  • Projects, tasks & per-project team assignment
  • Payroll runs & invoicing

Differentiators

  • Client portal + verifiable "Proof-of-Work" certificates
  • AI: auto-standups, insights, forecasting, "Ask FlowTrack"
  • Integrations: Slack, Jira, GitHub, Calendar
  • Role-based access, plan gating & Stripe billing
Status: Not a prototype. The platform is production-ready with billing, permissions, integrations and a developer API already shipped.
